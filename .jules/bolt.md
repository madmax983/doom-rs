**[Optimizing DemoPlayback API]**
**Learning:** `Vec<T>` cloning during demo playback on every tic can be replaced by yielding slices `&[T]`. Even though `Vec<Vec<DemoTicCmd>>` allocates in `next_tic_cmds` it isn't mutating `self.tics`, so an `&[DemoTicCmd]` or slice iterator is completely safe to return if lifetimes allow. In this case, `next_tic_cmds` could yield a slice, but we need to watch out for the borrow checker when the caller needs mutable access. I used `&[DemoTicCmd]` and refactored the method to return `Option<&[DemoTicCmd]>`. This eliminates a heap allocation every frame in DemoPlayback.
**Action:** When a method returns `Option<Vec<T>>` just by cloning from an internal vector, if the internal collection isn't mutated during the iteration, try returning `Option<&[T]>` instead. Ensure callers don't require `&mut self` on the struct while holding the reference. Here it worked gracefully.
