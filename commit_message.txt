⚡ Bolt: Pre-allocate VisplaneSet planes

💡 What: Changed `VisplaneSet::new()` to initialize its internal `planes` vector with `Vec::with_capacity(128)` instead of starting empty and dynamically reallocating.

🎯 Why: The wall rendering pass creates visplanes (ceiling/floor strips) continuously during the main hot loop. Growing `Vec::new()` requires multiple costly heap reallocations per frame, slowing down rendering.

📊 Impact: Eliminates almost all dynamic vector reallocations for visplanes during standard rendering loops. For typical Doom maps, 128 is a safe ceiling that easily covers visible sectors, turning O(log n) reallocations into 0 allocations on the hot path.

🔬 Measurement: Verified with `cargo test -p doom-renderer`.
