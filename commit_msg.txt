🗺️ Atlas: [architectural change] Resolve circular dependencies in doom-game

🕸️ Tangle: The `doom-game` crate contained three circular dependencies that violated the clean directed acyclic graph structure:
1. `random` <-> `state`: `random` functions like `p_damage_with_variance` took `&mut GameState` as an argument, while `state` imported `DoomRng` from `random`.
2. `spawn` <-> `state`: `spawn` used `&mut GameState`, and `state` imported the `Skill` enum from `spawn`.
3. `savegame` <-> `savegame_vanilla`: `savegame_vanilla` imported `SaveError` and `SaveGame` from `savegame`, while `savegame` imported `VanillaSaveHeader` from `savegame_vanilla`.

📐 Blueprint: The solution resolves these cycles:
- Shifted the PRNG helper methods (`p_random`, `p_random_range`, `p_subrandom`) directly into `DoomRng` in `random.rs`, and updated functions to take `&mut DoomRng` instead of `&mut GameState`, breaking the cycle.
- Moved the `Skill` enum from `spawn.rs` to `state.rs` since it's logically part of the global game state.
- Merged `savegame_vanilla.rs` entirely into `savegame.rs` to completely eliminate the module boundary cycle and consolidate the save functionality.

🧱 Stability: Reduced coupling, faster compile times, and eliminated cyclical `mod` boundaries.

🔬 Verification: Builds successfully, strict separation enforced. `cargo clippy`, `cargo test`, and `cargo fmt` complete without errors.
